FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What if I'm not tech-savvy?

No problem at all! Our system is designed to be user-friendly, and we provide step-by-step guidance. Many of our most successful clients started with very little technical knowledge. We can even handle the technical setup for you if needed.

How quickly will I see results?

Most of our clients start seeing new leads within the first week of implementation. For membership growth, you can typically expect significant results within 30-60 days, depending on your market and how quickly you implement our recommendations.

Does this work for all martial arts styles?

Yes! We've successfully worked with BJJ, MMA, Karate, Taekwondo, Kung Fu, Krav Maga, and many other types of martial arts schools. The principles of effective marketing apply across all styles, and we customize our approach to highlight the unique aspects of your specific discipline.

What budget do I need for advertising?

We recommend starting with at least £500/month for ad spend, but we'll work with your budget. What's most important is the ROI—our average client sees a return of 8.5x on their ad spend, turning every £1 into £8.50 of membership revenue.

What if I already have a website?

Great! We can work with your existing website and improve it for better conversion rates. If major changes are needed, we'll provide recommendations, but we always start by leveraging what you already have in place.

Is there any obligation after the free call?

None whatsoever. The strategy call is completely free and without obligation. You'll receive valuable insights you can implement whether you decide to work with us or not. Our goal is to provide so much value that you'll want to continue the relationship.
